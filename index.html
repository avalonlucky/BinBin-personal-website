我的价值观

我反复遵循的原则，塑造了我的思考方式、设计和协作方式

01

少即是多

剔除不必要的元素，只留下真正重要的部分。每个元素都因其用途而非装饰而获得存在的意义。

02

用户至上

一切都源于真实的人和真实的问题。同理心是这一切的基石。

03

把力气用在
对的地方

可扩展的系统、可复用的模式以及关键领域的自动化。追求效率，是对每个人时间的尊重。

04

良好的沟通

如果无法解释其背后的原因，再好的设计也毫无意义。清晰的沟通与清晰的屏幕显示同样重要。

FAQs

What types of projects do you take on?

We specialise in branding and UX/UI design for websites and apps. Whether it's a full brand identity, a design system build, or ongoing design support, we adapt to fit your business goals.

Which industries do you work with?

Our core expertise is in fintech and e-commerce, but we've partnered with clients across hospitality, law, accounting, packaging, edtech and many more.

How does your pricing work?

We offer both project-based pricing and ongoing retainers, billed at a flat hourly rate. Once we scope your project, you'll receive a clear estimate before we begin.

Do you handle development too?

We focus on what we do best: design. For development, we collaborate with trusted partners. If you already have a dev team, we'll work hand-in-hand with them.

What is your design process like?

We follow a structured yet flexible process: discovery and research, concept development, design, iteration, and handoff. Every stage involves you so there are no surprises at the end.

How long does a typical project take?

Branding projects take roughly 6–8 weeks, and websites or apps typically run 8–12 weeks from kickoff to development handover.
